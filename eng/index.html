Frequently asked questions about Sp. z o.o.

Can I switch my Sp. z o.o. accountant mid-year?+

Yes. We request the księgi rachunkowe and documents from your previous accountant, review the CIT and VAT calculations and continue the books from the month of transition. If your previous accountant is unresponsive, we'll advise you on how to reconstruct the records.

My Sp. z o.o. is dormant. Do I still need accounting?+

Yes — even a company with no turnover must file annual financial statements with KRS and tax returns. We offer a separate package for such companies with a minimal number of transactions.

How can the management board legally take money out of the company?+

There are several options: dividends, board member remuneration based on an uchwała or contract, and other forms — each with its own taxes and contributions. We choose the right structure for your situation and prepare the documents.

How do you work with KSeF?+

KSeF support is included in our accounting service: we help set up access and e-invoice issuing, and record incoming KSeF invoices in your books.

How long does it take to register a Sp. z o.o.?+

It depends on the registration method (online via S24 or traditional) and how long KRS takes to review the application. During a consultation we'll give you an estimated timeline for your case and prepare the documents to avoid rejections.
